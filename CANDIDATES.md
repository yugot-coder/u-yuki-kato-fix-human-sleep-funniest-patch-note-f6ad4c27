# Candidates — Fix Human Sleep: Funniest Patch Note

Gate (from kit `competition.yaml`): `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of
1 to 160 characters, not only white space, a single line. Judge (pairwise,
z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) weighs, in order: (1) really a patch note about fixing
human sleep, (2) funny on a single read, (3) genuine changelog voice,
(4) single line under the cap, (5) surprise — a specific, unexpected fix.

Known competitor lines to avoid imitating:
- "Fixed: REM sleep no longer waits out 45 minutes of imaginary arguments"
- "Fixed: human sleep no longer crashes at 3 a.m."

## 10 candidates

1. **Fixed: your brain no longer installs updates the moment your head hits the pillow.**
   (angle: update/install at bedtime)
2. Fixed: sleep now saves progress; waking up at 3 a.m. no longer restarts the whole night.
   (angle: save-game / progress)
3. Fixed: falling asleep no longer requires replaying every embarrassing moment since 2009.
   (angle: replaying awkward memories)
4. Fixed: snooze no longer negotiates a 9-minute extension to your dreams.
   (angle: snooze button)
5. Fixed: dreams now ship with a skip button for the exam you passed years ago.
   (angle: dreams / skip button)
6. Fixed: sleeping 8 hours no longer requires 10 hours of lying awake first.
   (angle: the 8-hour paradox)
7. Fixed: REM sleep no longer buffers while your brain loads tomorrow's to-do list.
   (angle: REM buffering)
8. Fixed: sleep mode no longer keeps your brain running at 100% all night.
   (angle: battery / power drain)
9. Fixed: waking up refreshed is no longer a beta feature available to other people only.
   (angle: beta feature)
10. Fixed: human sleep no longer crashes when you remember, mid-dream, that you left the oven on.
    (angle: mid-dream crash)

## Winner

**#1 — "Fixed: your brain no longer installs updates the moment your head hits the pillow."**

Why it wins:
- Reads exactly like a real changelog line: "Fixed:" + a concrete bug + a software metaphor (installing updates) applied to the body.
- The joke lands on first read and needs no explanation: everyone knows the feeling of the brain switching on the second the head touches the pillow.
- Fresh angle: no 3 a.m. crash, no REM/argument replay — it does not echo the known competitor lines.
- Specific and surprising (criterion 5): the fix is a precise, unexpected behaviour, not a flat "sleep is now fixed".
- Terse (83 characters), one line, well under the 160-character cap.
